- Led development of VLM pipeline (LLaVA 1.5-7B, Python) to classify property-level damage severity across 4000+ buildings from 68 satellite images on a team of 6, achieving 60.3% accuracy against FEMA ground-truth labels.
- Built React.js/Mapbox dashboard rendering building-level damage heatmaps over pre/post disaster imagery from AWS S3.
- Built OpenAI chatbot grounded in VLM damage data, responding to queries in chat and with live map actions/navigation.
